What we do

LVRI Athens is a centre for cosmetic gynaecology and reconstructive pelvic floor surgery, working with CO2 laser. It has been open in Athens since 2008. Consultations, examinations and in-office laser sessions take place at the practice in Vyronas; operations take place at REA Maternity Hospital, where Dr. Myrillas has directed the department of vaginal plastic and reconstructive pelvic laser surgery since 2013.

What we treat

Urinary incontinence, uterine and vaginal prolapse, cystocele, rectocele and enterocele, vaginal dryness and atrophy, and vaginal laxity — with options that run from a painless in-office laser session to full vaginal reconstruction.

Techniques

The practice works with Laser Vaginal Rejuvenation® and Designer Laser Vaginoplasty®, the techniques developed by Dr. David L. Matlock, alongside non-invasive laser rejuvenation and injectable treatments.
